Get Started

Click the button below to enter your monthly budget and expenses. SpendWise will calculate your remaining balance and show a full breakdown in your browser console.

Your Budget Summary

Open your browser console (F12 → Console tab) to see the full detailed breakdown of your budget calculations.

How it works

  1. You'll be prompted to enter your total monthly income/budget.
  2. You'll then enter several expenses one at a time.
  3. SpendWise calculates your total spending and remaining balance.
  4. Results are logged clearly to the console and shown on screen.
